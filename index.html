Care guidance

Simple habits. Safer decisions.

Use a consistent photography routine so visual comparisons are more meaningful.

01

Take consistent photos

Use similar lighting, distance, and angle each time. Avoid filters and keep the area in focus.

02

Keep the scale consistent

Use the same distance and angle each time. A future version should include a reference marker for physical area measurements.

03

Use professional judgment

Share your timeline with a clinician. Do not delay care while waiting for an application result.

Prototype limitation

Image analysis is not a diagnosis

This system supports visual progress tracking only. It cannot rule out complications, provide treatment recommendations, or replace professional assessment.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice or when concerned about a wound.
